When the evidence is incomplete, what should the agent do?

I study reliable and adaptive agents. The system has to decide whether to look again, what to carry to the next step, and when a person should answer.

  1. 01 · Look Uncertainty Retrieve again, open another modality, or stop?
  2. 02 · Carry State What should cross a reasoning edge, so an early error does not become the next context?
  3. 03 · Ask Help When should the agent ask, what should the question name, and how should the answer change the next task?

I am an M.S. student in Computer Science at Yale. Before that I finished a B.S. in Computer Science and a minor in Applied Mathematics at Kean (GPA 3.92, Summa Cum Laude), with a summer at Stanford.

The projects below are one line of work. Detection taught me that a score on a finished string does not choose the next action. Retrieval made the choice of where to look explicit. A document agent and a manuscript in preparation ask which part of a derivation the next step is allowed to see. Since September 2026 I have been in Professor Tesca Fitzgerald’s lab, on when a model should ask for help. That project is early. I am not reporting a result from it.

A short trace

  1. 01 · Under review · VLDB 2026

    Where to look

    First-author manuscript on cross-modal retrieval. Sinkhorn-balanced routing, R@1 53.6% on MS-COCO and R@5 46.7% on Visual News, while touching about 1.4% of the corpus and running about 13× faster end to end than GENIUS on Visual News.

  2. 02 · In preparation

    What the next step may see

    GARDS-RAG keeps the original question as a global anchor and passes only the predecessor’s subquestion, answer, and rationale along each edge. Preliminary results on MuSiQue, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and HotpotQA are ahead of iterative and graph baselines. The manuscript is in preparation.

  3. 03 · Yale · since September 2026

    When to ask

    With Professor Tesca Fitzgerald, I am exploring when a vision-language-action model should ask for help after a failure, and how a correction should change the next attempt.

  4. Published · CSCI 2025

    A score is not a next action

    First author. A BERT model and a random forest reached 99.9% F1 on abstracts and 93.5% F1 on sentences. Attributions concentrated on perplexity and log-probability. The detector scores a finished string.
