Lining Yan

I am an M.S. student in Computer Science at Yale University.

My research interests center on robotic learning, multimodal retrieval, efficient deep learning, agentic workflows, and NLP. I am interested in how intelligent systems can learn from limited supervision, retrieve across modalities, and act reliably in interactive settings.

Before Yale, I completed my B.S. in Computer Science with a minor in Applied Mathematics at Kean University (GPA 3.92, Summa Cum Laude), and spent a summer at Stanford studying high-performance computing and data analysis. I have conducted research at Wenzhou-Kean University, the National Research Institute in Beijing, and the University of Notre Dame.
