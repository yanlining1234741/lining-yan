Research

The same three questions run through the work. Each piece is marked published, under review, in preparation, or early.

The line I want to continue

  • 01 Look Under review · VLDB 2026 · First author

    Bridging the Modality Gap that Hurts Cross-Modal Retrieval

    With Dr. Guoxin Kang. Semantic bucket assignment as entropy-regularized optimal transport, Sinkhorn-balanced routing, bucket-local filtering, and exact cosine reranking. R@1 of 53.6% on MS-COCO and R@5 of 46.7% on Visual News, exploring about 1.4% of the corpus, with about a 13-fold end-to-end speedup over GENIUS on Visual News.

  • 02 Carry In preparation

    GARDS-RAG

    The original question stays a global anchor. Each edge of a DAG passes only the predecessor’s subquestion, answer, and rationale. Preliminary results on MuSiQue, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and HotpotQA outperform iterative and graph-structured baselines. This is a manuscript in preparation, not an accepted paper.

  • 03 Ask Yale · September 2026 – present

    Help-seeking in Professor Tesca Fitzgerald’s lab

    Exploring when a vision-language-action model should ask for help after a failure, and how a correction should change the next attempt. Early. No result to report.

  • 03 Ask Notre Dame · Summer 2026

    Document agent for scientific evidence

    With Dr. Samuel Rund. An extractor for study design, sample size, population, intervention, and outcome from prose, tables, and figures. The checks bind a metric to the experiment, test completeness, separate Methods from Results, and repair a false “Not stated.”
